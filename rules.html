TECHNOVA Rules

Please read the rules and guidelines carefully before registering.

📌 General Rules

  1. Participants must register for the events before the registration deadline.
  2. Participants must carry their college ID card during the event.
  3. Participants should report to the venue before the scheduled event time.
  4. Participants must follow the instructions given by the event coordinators.
  5. Any form of malpractice or unfair means will lead to disqualification.
  6. Participants are responsible for their personal belongings.
  7. Participants must maintain discipline and proper behaviour throughout the fest.
  8. The decision of the judges and event coordinators will be final.

📝 Registration Rules

  1. Participants must provide correct and valid information during registration.
  2. Each participant should register only for the events they wish to participate in.
  3. Technical Quiz and Paper Presentation allow a maximum of 2 participants per team.
  4. Debugging, Web Designing and Rapid Fire are individual events.
  5. Participants should check their registration details before submitting the form.

🎯 Event Rules

💡 Technical Quiz

  • Maximum of 2 participants per team.
  • Questions will be based on Computer Science and technology.
  • Participants must follow the time limit given by the coordinators.

📄 Paper Presentation

  • Maximum of 2 participants per team.
  • Participants must present their selected technical topic.
  • Presentation must be completed within the given time limit.
  • Participants should bring their presentation material as instructed.

🐞 Debugging

  • This is an individual event.
  • Participants will be given programs containing errors.
  • Participants must identify and correct the errors within the given time.

💻 Web Designing

  • This is an individual event.
  • Participants will be given a web designing task.
  • The website should be created within the given time.
  • Participants must follow the instructions provided by the coordinators.

⚡ Rapid Fire

  • This is an individual event.
  • Participants will answer questions within a limited time.
  • Participants should answer independently.
  • The coordinator's decision will be final.

⚠️ Important Note

Participants are requested to arrive at the venue on time and follow all instructions given by the event coordinators. Any violation of the rules may result in disqualification.
